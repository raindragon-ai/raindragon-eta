RainDragon Forecast

Forecast shows how long a reply usually takes you, as a range from your own past replies. Everything stays in this browser.

Off hides the forecast and stops recording. Your history is kept.
Until 30 replies the wider range is used and marked "still learning".
Reads status.claude.com (on claude.ai) and status.openai.com (on chatgpt.com) and Google's status dashboard, www.google.com/appsstatus (on gemini.google.com), every 2 minutes, and shows a storm warning during a major incident.
Clear removes the claude.ai, chatgpt.com and gemini.google.com histories. Diagnostics hold the version, settings and counts only: no prompts, replies or chat names.
